--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ Z AXIS 1 & Z AXIS 2 ]

  - Active focused window is positioned at the front (scale 1.0, opacity 1.0).
  - Background windows are scaled down (e.g., 0.8, 0.6) and faded.
  - Alt+Tab / Logo switcher Z-scroll cycles windows dynamically.
  - Wrapped focus allows seamless continuous rotation from Z AXIS 2 back to 1.

[ FULLSCREEN ]
  - Clients requesting fullscreen bypass tiling.
  - The compositor centers and resizes the client to cover the output viewport.
  - Camera viewport is locked or panned accordingly.

[ HALF SIZE ]
  - Under Grid or Diagonal tiling modes, new columns default to half size.
  - Gaps (default 10px) and margins (default 20px) are calculated to fill width.

[ WINDOW 1, WINDOW 2, & WINDOW 3 ]
  - Represent columns or tabs in a stacked tab group.
  - Window 1: Left column or bottom/background tab.
  - Window 2: Middle column or focused foreground tab.
  - Window 3: Right column.

[ WORKSPACER ]
  - Displays workspaces as distinct rows spaced Vertically.
  - Overview Mode scales the entire Workspacer layer down to 0.45 size.
  - Workspace switchers slide coordinates vertically to target viewports.
